## tldr

i work on human-AI interfaces, and the environments driving them.

i’m interested in how interfaces are evolving, the seams disappearing, and models generating visual experiences. i follow the research and discussions around this, and tinker in the space.

i also like building tools that handle the repetitive decisions, so i can spend more time on the rules and relationships behind them. part of the appeal is changing how things fit together and seeing what comes out. and sometimes, you get unexpected wonder.

today i work on agentic products at block. ranging from design capabilities, to builder tools, to design infrastructure powering cash app.

i appreciate a good drive with music (more often than not, synthwave), and go climbing when i can. i read a lot of science fiction and occasionally try writing it. my partner and i want to buy land with a stream someday.
