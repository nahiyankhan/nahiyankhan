## tldr

i work on human-AI interfaces, and the capabilities driving them. i’m interested in how interfaces are evolving, the seams disappearing, and models generating visual experiences. i follow the research and discussions around this.

i like building tools that handle the repetitive decisions, so i can spend more time on the rules and relationships behind them. i tend to figure things out as i build, and the quirks and wrinkles along the way are part of that. they help me understand what i’m making and where i want to take it.

today i work on agentic products at block. ranging from design capabilities, to builder tools, to design infrastructure powering cash app.

i appreciate a good drive with music (more often than not, synthwave), and go climbing when i can. i read a lot of science fiction and occasionally try writing it. my partner and i want to buy land with a stream someday.
